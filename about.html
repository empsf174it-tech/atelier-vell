About the Maison

Our Heritage

Atelier Vell was born from a desire to strip away the unnecessary, focusing entirely on structural integrity, exquisite materials, and masterful construction.

Philosophy

Every stitch is considered. Every edge is hand-painted. The result is a piece that transcends seasonal trends.

The Process

Made by hand, slowly

We work exclusively with multi-generational family tanneries in Tuscany, selecting only the finest full-grain leathers that develop a unique patina over time.

Each bag passes through the hands of a single artisan from cutting to final finishing, so every piece carries the signature of the person who made it.

The People

Behind the Maison

Three disciplines, one bench — the hands and minds that shape every Atelier Vell piece.

Founder & Creative Director

Elena Vell

Trained in architecture before turning to leather, Elena draws every silhouette by hand and signs off each prototype herself.

Master Artisan

Marco Bellandi

Third-generation leatherworker from Florence. Marco leads the bench, teaching the hand-stitching and edge-painting our pieces are known for.

Head of Materials

Sofia Ricci

Sofia works directly with our Tuscan tanneries, selecting every hide by hand for grain, weight and the way it will age.
