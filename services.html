At Your Service

Client Services

Everything you need to know about purchasing, shipping, and caring for your Atelier Vell pieces.

01 — Delivery

Shipping & Returns

Complimentary Global Shipping

We offer complimentary express shipping on all orders worldwide. Orders are processed within 1-2 business days and typically arrive within 3-5 business days via DHL Express.

Return Policy

We gladly accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in their original, unused condition with all tags and packaging intact. A prepaid return label is included in every shipment for your convenience.

02 — Aftercare

Care & Repair

Our leathers are selected for their natural beauty and will develop a unique patina over time. To maintain the integrity of your bag:

  • Store in the provided dust bag when not in use.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and moisture.
  • Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth.

We offer a complimentary repair service for structural defects within the first two years of purchase.

03 — Questions

Frequently Asked

Can't find what you're looking for? Our client advisors reply within one business day.

Ask a Question
How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and typically arrive within 3–5 business days via express courier, with tracking sent by email.

Can I exchange a piece for a different colour?

Yes. Within 30 days of delivery, unused pieces can be exchanged for another colour or style. Simply contact client services and we'll arrange the collection.

Do you offer personalisation?

Most bags can be hot-stamped with up to three initials in gold, silver or blind embossing. Personalised pieces are final sale.

How should I care for full-grain leather?

Store your bag stuffed and in its dust bag, away from direct sunlight. Wipe with a soft dry cloth and condition two to three times a year.

What does the repair service cover?

Structural defects such as stitching, hardware and handle attachments are repaired free of charge for two years from purchase.
